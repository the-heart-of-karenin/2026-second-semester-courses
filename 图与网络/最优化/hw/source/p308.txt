Proposition 13.25. Any independence system (E, F) is the intersection of a ﬁnite
number of matroids.
Proof: Each circuit C of (E, F) deﬁnes a matroid (E, {F ⊆ E : C \ F ̸=∅ } )
by Theorem 13.12. The intersection of all these matroids is of course (E, F). 2
