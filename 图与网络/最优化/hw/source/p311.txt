Proposition 13.29. Let (E, F1) and (E, F2) be two matroids with rank functions
r1 and r2. Then for any F ∈ F1 ∩ F2 and any Q ⊆ E we have
|F|≤ r1(Q) + r2(E \ Q).
Proof: F ∩ Q ∈ F1 implies |F ∩ Q|≤ r1(Q). Similarly F \ Q ∈ F2 implies
|F \ Q|≤ r2(E \ Q). Adding the two inequalities completes the proof. 2
