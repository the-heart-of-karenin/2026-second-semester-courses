§20. Abel's Theorem 
In this section we investigate when there exist meromorphic functions with 
prescribed zeros and poles on a compact Riemann surface X. Clearly it is 
necessary that the total order of the zeros equal the total order of the poles. 
However on Riemann surfaces of genus g ~ 1 this condition is not sufficient. 
Abel's Theorem gives a necessary and sufficient condition for the existence of 
such functions.
