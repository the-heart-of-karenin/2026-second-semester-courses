27.12. Theorem 
(a) The Riemann sphere pI is elliptic. 
(b) The complex plane e, the punctured plane e* and all tori e/r are 
parabolic. 
(c) Every other Riemann surface is hyperbolic. 
Thus, in particular, a compact Riemann surface is elliptic, parabolic or 
hyperbolic depending on whether its genus is zero, one or greater than one.
