§17. The Serre Duality Theorem 
The Serre Duality Theorem allows a simpler interpretation of the cohomo-
logy groups Hl(X, (DD) in terms of differential forms. In fact, dim Hl(X, (DD) 
is equal to the maximum number of linearly independent meromorphic 
1-forms which are multiples of the divisor D. One consequence is the 
Riemann-Hurwitz formula, which allows one to calculate the genus of a 
covering from the number of sheets it has and its branching order. Another 
consequence is a vanishing theorem which asserts that Hl(X, (DD) = 0, if 
deg D > 2g - 2. This vanishing theorem itself has interesting applications 
and we will use it to prove an embedding theorem for compact Riemann
