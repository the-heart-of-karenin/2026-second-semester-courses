The book of Hermann Weyl, which first appeared in 1913 (in German), was the 
first modern presentation of the theory of Riemann surfaces. It is still well worth 
reading and contains many references to the older literature, particularly that of the 
nineteenth century. The books of Chevalley and Serre treat the theory of Riemann 
surfaces from the algebraic standpoint.
