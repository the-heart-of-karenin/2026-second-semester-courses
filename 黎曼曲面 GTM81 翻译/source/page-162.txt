§19. Harmonic Differential Forms 
With the help of the results obtained so far it is now easy to derive the most 
important results about harmonic differential forms on compact Riemann 
surfaces X. In particular every closed differential form on X may be uniquely 
written as the sum of a harmonic and an exact differential form. This implies 
that the 1st deRham group of X is isomorphic to the vector space of har-
monic differential forms on X. Using this one can show that the genus is a 
topological invariant.
