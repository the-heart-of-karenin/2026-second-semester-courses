§24. Weyl's Lemma 
In this section we introduce the notion of distributions. These are gener-
alized functions. In the class of distributions differentiation is possible with-
out any restrictions. Hence it is possible to consider solutions of differential 
equations in the sense of distributions. Now Weyl's Lemma asserts that for 
Laplace's equation du = 0 both kinds of solutions are the same, i.e., every 
harmonic distribution is a smooth function in the usual sense which satisfies 
Laplace's equation.
