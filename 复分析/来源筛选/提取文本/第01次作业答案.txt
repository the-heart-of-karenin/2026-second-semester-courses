English solution. Under R2 ∼= C, the matrix J represents multiplication by i. A real-linear

map A = JF is complex-linear exactly when A(iw) = iA(w) for every w, or equivalently AJ =
JA. The displayed products show that this commutation relation is precisely ux = vy and
uy = −vx. If f is holomorphic, its real differential is h 7→f′(z)h, which is complex-linear. Thus

its Jacobian commutes with J, and the matrix identity is equivalent to the Cauchy–Riemann

equations.

  3. Laplace 算子与Wirtinger 导数/ Laplacian and Wirtinger

                            derivatives


题目/ Problem. 证明∆= 4∂z∂¯z。Show that ∆= 4∂z∂¯z.

中文解答. 对任意C2 函数，定义

                             1                   1
                           ∂z = 2(∂x −i∂y),       ∂¯z = 2(∂x + i∂y).

由于混合偏导可交换，

                           4∂z∂¯z = (∂x −i∂y)(∂x + i∂y) = ∂2x + ∂2y = ∆.

English solution. For a C2 function, expand the product of the two Wirtinger operators as

above. The mixed terms cancel because ∂x∂y = ∂y∂x, leaving 4∂z∂¯z = ∆.
