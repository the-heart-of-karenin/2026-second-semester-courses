1. If {C, ϵ} is an augmented chain complex, show that

2. Use the cellular chain complexes to compute the homology, with
general coefficients G, of the following spaces:
(a) T # T.
(b) P2 # P2 # P2.
(c) PN.
(d) the k-fold dunce cap.
3. Prove theorem 6.3.2.
4. Let R be a commutative ring with unity. Let C be a chain complex.
Show that Cp ⊗R can be given the structure of an R-module by
defining
α(cp ⊗β) = cp ⊗(αβ)
for α, β ∈R. Show that Hp(C; R) has the structure of an R-module,
and that chain maps induce R-module homomorphisms. Show that
∂∗is an R-module homomorphism.
