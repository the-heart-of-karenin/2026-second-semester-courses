Let us now consider the homomorphism of cohomology induced by a sim-
plicial map.
Recall that if f : (K, K0) →(L, L0) is a simplicial map, then one has a
corresponding chain map
f# : Cp(K, K0) →Cp(L, L0) .
The dual of f# maps cochains to cochains; we usually denote it by f #. Because
f# commutes with ∂, the map f # commutes with δ. The dual of the equation
f# ◦∂= ∂◦f# is the equation δ ◦f # = f # ◦δ. Hence, f # carries cocycles to
cocycles and coboundaries to coboundaries. It is called a cochain map, and it
induces a homomorphism of cohomology groups
Hp(K, K0; G) f ∗
←−Hp(L, L0; G) .
Functoriality holds, even on the cochain level. For if i is the identity map,
then i# is the identity and so is i#. Similarly, the equation (g ◦f)# = g# ◦f#,
when dualized, gives the equation (g ◦f)# = f # ◦g#.
