Theorem 8.13.16 Let X be a compact, triangulable space. Let D1 ⊃D2 ⊃
· · · be a sequence of polyhedra in X whose intersection is Y . Then
ˇHk(Y ; G) ∼= dir lim Hk(Dn; G) .
The same result holds in reduced cohomology.
Proof: We shall omit G from the notation for simplicity. The direct limit in
the conclusion of the theorem does not depend on the particular triangulations
of the spaces Dn. Let Xn be a triangulation of X chosen so that Dn is the
polytope of a full subcomplex of Xn, and so that the maximum diameter of
a simplex of Xn is less than 1/n. And so that the inclusion map |Xn+1| →
|Xn| satisfies the star condition. Let us denote the subcomplex of Xn whose
polytope is Dn by Kn. Then the isomorphism
dir lim Hk(Dn) ∼= dir lim Hk(Kn)
holds trivially.
