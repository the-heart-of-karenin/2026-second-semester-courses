3. Let G and H be the functors assigning to a complex K its oriented
chain complex and its ordered chained complex, respectively. To
each simplicial map they assign the induced chain map.
(a) There is a natural transformation either of G to H or of H to
G. Which?
(b) Show that if you consider G and H as taking values in the cat-
egory of chain complexes and chain homotopy classes of chain
maps, then both natural transformations exist and are natural
equivalences.
