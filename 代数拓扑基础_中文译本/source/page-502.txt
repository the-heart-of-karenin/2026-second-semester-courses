The proof of this last theorem generalizes to prove the ham sandwich
theorem, that is, to show that for n bounded measurable sets in Rn, there is
an n −1 plane in Rn that bisects them all. The motivation for the name of
the ham sandwich theorem is the following: One thinks of a ham sandwich
as consisting of two slices of bread and a slice of ham, so in case n = 3, the
theorem says that one can bisect each slice of bread and the slice of ham with
a single whack of the knife.
