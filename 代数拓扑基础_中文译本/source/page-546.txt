Alexander–Pontryagin Duality
Now we prove a generalized version of the Alexander duality theorem. This
version applies to an arbitrary closed subspace of Sn, not just to a polyhedron
in Sn. As an application, we derive a version of the Jordan curve theorem
more general than any we have seen up until now.
