Let p : X ∪Y →X ∪f Y be the quotient map. We show first that p defines
a homeomorphism of Y with a closed subspace of X ∪f Y . Obviously, p|Y
is continuous and injective. Furthermore, if C is closed in Y , then f −1(C) is

Now if X and Y are T1-spaces, then X ∪f Y is also a T1-space, since each
of the equivalence classes is closed in X ∪Y . The preceding theorem does not
apply to give us further separation properties, for p is not, in general, a closed
map. Nonetheless, we can still prove the following.
Theorem 4.11.3 If X and Y are normal, then the adjunction space X ∪f Y
is normal.
