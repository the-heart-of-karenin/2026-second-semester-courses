EXAMPLE 4.10.2 Let X be a subset of euclidean space, and let γ1 and γ2
be loops in X. Suppose the line segment from γ1(t) to γ2(t) is contained in
X, for each 0 ≤t ≤1, then γ1 ≃γ2. You have probably already realized that
the homotopy function is
Γ(t, u) = (1 −u) γ1(t) + u γ2(t) .
