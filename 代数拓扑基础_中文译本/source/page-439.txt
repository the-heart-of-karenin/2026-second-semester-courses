Theorem 7.9.2 (The Eilenberg–Zilber theorem) For every pair X, Y
of topological spaces, there are chain maps

that are chain-homotopy inverse to each other. The maps are natural with
respect to chain maps induced by continuous maps.
Proof: Consider the category of pairs of topological spaces and pairs of con-
tinuous maps. We define two functors from this category to the category of
augmented chain complexes as follows:
G(X, Y ) = S(X) ⊗S(Y ) ,
G′(X, Y ) = S(X × Y ) ,
G(f, g) = f# ⊗g# ,
G′(f, g) = (f × g)# .
It is easy to check the functorial properties.
Let M be the collection of all pairs (∆p, ∆q), where ∆k denotes the stan-
dard k-simplex. We show that G and G′ are both acyclic and free relative to
the collection of models M. Then the theorem follows immediately from the
acyclic model theorem [Theorem 4.5.5].
