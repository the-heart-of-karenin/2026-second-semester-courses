Corollary 7.5.2 Let (X, A) be a topological pair. Then there is an exact se-
quence
0 −→Hp(X, A) ⊗G −→Hp(X, A; G) −→Hp−1(X, A) ∗G −→0 ,
that is natural with respect to homomorphisms induced by chain maps. It splits,
but not naturally.
Corollary 7.5.3 Let C and D be free chain complexes, and let ϕ : C →D be a
chain map. If ϕ∗: Hi(C) →Hi(D) is an isomorphism for i = p and i = p −1,
then
ϕ∗: Hp(C; G) →Hp(D; G)
is an isomorphism for arbitrary G.
Proof: This result follows from naturality of the universal coefficient sequence
and the Steenrod five lemma [Lemma 3.3.4].
This last corollary provides an alternative proof of Theorem 6.3.1.

general coefficients G, of the following spaces:
(a) T # T.
(b) P2 # P2 # P2.
(b) PN.
(c) the k-fold dunce cap.
