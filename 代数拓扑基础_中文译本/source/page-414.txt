One property possessed by the torsion product that is not possessed by
Ext is commutativity. To prove it, we need the following lemma.
Lemma 7.4.4 There is a function assigning to each short exact sequence of
abelian groups
0 −→A −→B −→C −→0
and each abelian group D, an exact sequence
0 −→D ∗A −→D ∗B −→D ∗C −→D ⊗A −→D ⊗B −→D ⊗C −→0 .
This function is natural with respect to homomorphisms of short exact se-
quences and abelian groups.
