h(x) = h(−x) for at least one x ∈Sn.

Theorem 8.8.10 (Baby ham sandwich theorem) Let A1 and A2 be two
bounded, measurable subsets of R2. There is a line in R2 that bisects both A1
and A2.
Remark 8.8.11 This result is not elementary. Try finding such a line even
in the simple case where A1 and A2 are both triangular regions.
