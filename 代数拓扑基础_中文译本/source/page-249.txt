The left-hand side of (∗) is a singular homology group, but the right-hand

at most n. Therefore, this group vanishes in simplicial homology for p > n, so
it vanishes in singular homology as well.
The next three definitions are used in the exercises.
Definition 4.8.15 A space X is homogeneous if given x and y, there is a
homeomorphism of X with itself carrying x to y.
Definition 4.8.16 An n-dimensional homology sphere [respectively, homol-
ogy ball ] is a space or complex having the same homology groups as Sn [re-
spectively, Bn].
Definition 4.8.17 A solid torus is a space homeomorphic to S1 ×B2. Thus a
solid torus is a 3-manifold with boundary, and the boundary is homeomorphic
to the torus.
