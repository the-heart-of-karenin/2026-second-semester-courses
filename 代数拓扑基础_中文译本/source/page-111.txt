Application to Relative Homology
The preceding results about simplicial approximation generalize to relative
homology in a straightforward manner:
Lemma 2.2.7 Let h : |K| →|L| satisfy the star condition relative to K and
L. Suppose that h maps |K0| to |L0|, where K0 and L0 are subcomplexes.
Then
(a) Any simplicial approximation f : K →L to the map h also
maps |K0| to |L0|. Also the restriction of f to K0 is a simplicial
approximation to the restriction of h to |K0|.
