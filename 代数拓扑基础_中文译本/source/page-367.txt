p-simplex on X. Similarly, T ◦ℓ(ϵp, ϵp+1, . . . , ϵp+q) is the restriction of T to
the “back q-face” of ∆p+q. It is a singular q-simplex on X. The multiplication
indicated on the right-hand side of (∗) is multiplication in the ring R.
What this mysterious formula means remains to be seen.
Theorem 5.9.5 The cup product of cochains is bilinear and associative. The
cochain z0 whose value is 1 on each singular 0-simplex acts as a unity element.
Furthermore, the following coboundary formula holds:
δ(cp ⌣cq) = (δcp) ⌣cq + (−1)pcp ⌣(δcq)
