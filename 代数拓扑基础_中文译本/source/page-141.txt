Homotopy equivalences are difficult to visualize in general. There is a spe-
cial type of homotopy equivalence that is geometrically easier to understand.

One can visualize a deformation retraction as a gradual collapsing of the
space X onto the subspace A, such that each point of A remains fixed during
the process of collapse. This type of homotopy equivalence can thus be visu-
alized geometrically. It is intuitively clear that if A is a deformation retract of
X and B is a deformation retract of A, then B is a deformation retract of X.
This is also easy to prove.
