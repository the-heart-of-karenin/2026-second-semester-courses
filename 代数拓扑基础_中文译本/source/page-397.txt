7.2
The Ext Functor
Associated with the functor Hom (A, B) is another functor of two variables
called Ext (A, B). Like the Hom functor, it is contravariant in the first variable
and covariant in the second variable. This means that, given homomorphisms
γ : A →A′ and δ : B′ →B, there is a homomorphism
Ext (γ, δ) : Ext (A′, B′) →Ext (A, B) ,
and the usual functorial properties hold.
