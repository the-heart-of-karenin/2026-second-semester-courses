7.5
The Universal Coefficient Theorem for
Homology
As with the cohomology group Hp(X; G), there is a theorem expressing
the homology group Hp(X; G) in terms of the homology groups Hp(X) and
Hp−1(X). The statements of the two theorems are similar, except that here
the tensor product and the torsion product replace the Hom and Ext functors,
and the arrows are reversed.
Theorem 7.5.1 (The universal coefficient theorem for homology)
Let C be a free chain complex, and let G be an abelian group. There is an exact
sequence
0 −→Hp(C) ⊗G −→Hp(C; G) −→Hp−1(C) ∗G −→0 ,
that is natural with respect to homomorphisms induced by chain maps. It splits,
but not naturally.
