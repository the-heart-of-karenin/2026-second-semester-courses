Tensor Products of Modules
Let R be a commutative ring with unity element. If A and B are modules
over R, then as was mentioned in the exercises of Section 5.2, the group
HomR(A, B) of all module homomorphisms of A into B has the structure of
an R-module. An analogous situation obtains for the tensor product functor.
We shall in fact be interested only in the special case where R is a field, but
we may as well consider the general case for the time being.
Let A and B be modules over the ring R. As before, let F(A, B) be the
free abelian group generated by the set A × B. But now we let the relations
subgroup R(A, B) be generated by elements of the form
(a + a′, b) −(a, b) −(a′, b) ,
(a, b + b′) −(a, b) −(a, b′) ,
(αa, b) −(a, αb) ,
for α ∈R .
Then F(A, B)/R(A, B) has the structure of a module over R when, for α ∈R,
we define the map of F(A, B) to itself by α(a, b) = (αa, b). This map carries
R(A, B) into itself. To see this, observe that when we apply α ∈R to the first
of the listed generators for R(A, B), we have
(α(a + a′), b) −(αa, b) −(αa′, b)
= (αa + αa′, b) −(αa, b) −(αa′, b) .
