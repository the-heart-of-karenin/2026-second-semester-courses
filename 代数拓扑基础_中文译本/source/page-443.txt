7.10
∗The K¨unneth Theorem for Cohomolgy
One can always compute the cohomology of a product space by first computing
its homology from the K¨unneth theorem, and then applying the universal
coefficient theorem for cohomology. But one might hope for a version of the
K¨unneth theorem that applies directly to cohomology. We shall prove such a
theorem in this section. It has applications to computing the cohomology ring
of a product space, which we shall study in the next section.
First we introduce the cross product operation in cohomology. We give
only the definition here. Discussion of its properties will be postponed to the
next section.
Throughout this section, let R be a commutative ring with unity element.

there is a cochain complex Hom (C, R) with p-dimensional group Hom (Cp, R).
