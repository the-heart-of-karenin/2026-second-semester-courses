What remains to be shown is that ϵ is independent of the choice of α
and β. Suppose {α′, β′, γ} is another homomorphism of the two given free
resolutions. Consider the free resolution of A as a chain complex A, indexed
so that A is the 0-dimensional group. Do the same for A′, thus obtaining
a chain complex A′. Then {α, β, γ} and {α′, β′, γ} are chain maps of A to
A′. By exactness, the homology groups of A and A′ vanish. The cohomology
groups need not vanish. Indeed, the group cok ϕ is just the 2-dimensional
cohomology group H2(A; B), and similarly cok ϕ′ = H2(A′; B). The map ϵ is
just the cohomology homomorphism induced by the chain map {α, β, γ}.
We see that the hypotheses of Lemma 5.7.1 are satisfied by the chain com-
plexes A and A′. Therefore there is a chain homotopy D between the chain
map {α, β, γ} and the chain map {α′, β′, γ}. Then Hom (D, δ) is a cochain ho-
motopy between the corresponding cochain maps. It follows that they induce
the same homomorphism ϵ of 2-dimensional cohomology groups.
