EXERCISES
1. Let K be the complex pictured in Figure 1.10.2; let K1 be its outer
edge. Compute Hi(K) and Hi(K, K1).
2. Let K be a complex whose underlying space is the M¨obius band; let
K0 be its edge. Compute Hi(K) and Hi(K, K1). [Hint: See Exam-
ple 1.4.6. Here the edge consists of the line segments ab, bc, cd, de, ef,
and fa.]
3. Show that if K is a complex and v is a vertex of K, then Hi(K, v) ∼=
Hi(K) for all i. [Hint: Care is needed when i = 1.]
4. Describe H0(K, K0) in general.
5. Let |K| be the torus, represented by a labeled rectangle in the usual
way; let K0 be the subcomplex represented by the top edge of the
rectangle. Compute Hi(K, K0). [See Figure 1.7.4; |K0| is the union
of the line segments ab, bc, and ca.]
6. Let K be a 2-dimensional complex; let σ be a 2-simplex of K; let K0
be the subcomplex whose space is |K| \ Int σ. Compute Hi(K, K0).
