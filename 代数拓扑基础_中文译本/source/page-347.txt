Definition 5.6.1 A short exact sequence of abelian groups,
0 −→A −→B −→C −→0 ,
where A and B are free, is called a free resolution of C. Any abelian group C
has a free resolution: one can take B to be the free abelian group generated
by the elements of C, and take A to be the kernel of the natural projection
B −→C. This last construction gives what is known as the canonical free
resolution of C.
