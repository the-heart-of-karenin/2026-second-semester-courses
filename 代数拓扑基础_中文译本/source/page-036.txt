Definition 1.4.11 If L is a complex, a subcomplex L0 of L is said to be a
full subcomplex of L provided each simplex of L whose vertices belong to L0
belongs to L0 itself.
For example, the boundary of the rectangle L pictured in Figure 1.4.5 is
a full subcomplex of L, but the boundary of the rectangle pictured in Fig-
ure 1.4.6 is not.
Lemma 1.4.12 Let L be a complex; let f be a labeling of its vertices; let
g : |L| →|K| be the associated pasting map. Let L0 be a full subcomplex of L.
Suppose that whenever v and w are vertices of L having the same label:
(1) v and w belong to L0.
(2) St v and St w are disjoint.
