Now we apply these results to study local properties of arbitrary simplicial
complexes.
Definition 8.2.8 Let s be a simplex of the complex K. The star of s in K,
denoted by St s, is the union of the interiors of all simplices of K having s as
a face. The closure of St s is denoted by St s. It is the union of all simplices
of K having s as a face and is called the closed star of s in K. The link of s
in K, denoted by Lk s, is the union of all simplices of K lying in St s that are
disjoint from s.
We have already given these definitions when s is a vertex. See Section 1.3.
In general, if s = v0v1 . . . vn, then St s is the open subset of K consist-
ing of those points x for which the barycentric coordinate of x on each of
v0, v1, . . . , vn is positive. That is,
St s = St v0 ∩St v1 ∩· · · ∩St vn .
Observe that St s consists of all simplices of K of the form s ∗t. Also the
link of s equals the union of all the faces t of such simplices. The sets St s and
Lk s are thus polytopes of subcomplexes of K. We often use the notation St s
and Lk s to denote these complexes as well as their polytopes.
