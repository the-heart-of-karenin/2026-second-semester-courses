Note that here we are considering s and Bd s and Lk s not only as subspaces
of |K| but also as subcomplexes of K. In order that these equations should hold
even if Lk s or Bd s is empty, we make the convention that K ∗∅= ∅∗K = K

Proof: The first equation is immediate from the definitions. Also St s is the
union of all simplices of K of the form s ∗t. And Lk s is the union of all the
faces t of such simplices.
On the other hand, if a simplex of K lies in St s but not in St s, then it
must be a face of s ∗t of the form s′ ∗t′, where s′ is a proper face of s and t′
is a face of t. Since t lies in Lk s, so does t′. Hence s′ ∗t′ lies in Bd s ∗Lk s.
Conversely, every simplex of this form lies in St s but not in St s.
