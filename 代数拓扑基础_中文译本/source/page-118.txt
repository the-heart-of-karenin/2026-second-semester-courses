The next definition is used in Exercises 2 and 3.
Definition 2.3.12
(1) If A and B are collections of sets, we say that B refines A if for
each B ∈B there is an A ∈A such that B ⊂A.
(2) A space X is said to have finite covering dimension if there
is an integer m satisfying the following condition: For every open
covering A of X there is an open covering B of X that refines A
and such that no point of X lies in more than m + 1 elements of B.
(3) The covering dimension of such a space X is the least integer
m for which the condition in (2) holds.
