Using the results of (1)–(4), we can compute the homology of T. Every
1-cycle of T is homologous to a 1-cycle of the form nw1 + mz1, by (1) and
(3). Such a cycle bounds only if it is trivial: For if c = ∂d for some d, then
(2) applies to show that d = qγ. Since ∂γ = 0 by (4), we have c = ∂d = 0.
We conclude that
H1(T) ∼= Z ⊕Z ,
and the (cosets of the) 1-cycles w1 and z1 form a basis for the 1-dimensional
homology.
