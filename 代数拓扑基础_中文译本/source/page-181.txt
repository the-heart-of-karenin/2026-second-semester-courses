Theorem 3.4.1 Let K be a complex. Let K0 and K1 be subcomplexes such
that K = K0 ∪K1. Let A = K0 ∩K1. Then there is an exact sequence
· · · −→Hp(A) −→Hp(K0) ⊕Hp(K1) −→Hp(K) →Hp−1(A) −→· · · .
There is a similar exact sequence in reduced homology if A is nonempty.
Definition 3.4.2 The sequence in this last theorem is called the Mayer–
Vietoris sequence of (K0, K1).
