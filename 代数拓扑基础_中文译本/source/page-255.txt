Classically, the Jordan curve theorem2 is usually stated for spheres imbed-
ded in Rn rather than Sn. We prove that version of the theorem now.
Corollary 4.9.6 Let n ≥2. Let C be a subset of Rn homeomorphic to Sn−1.
Then Rn \ C has precisely two components, of which C is the common topo-
logical boundary.
