Definition 8.10.11 Let M be an n-manifold with boundary. We say that
Bd M has a product neighborhood in M if there is a homeomorphism
h : Bd M × [0, 1) →U
whose image is an open set in M and such that h(x, 0) = x for each x ∈Bd M.
It is in fact true that such a product neighborhood always exists, but the
proof is decidedly non-trivial [see [Bro62]].
