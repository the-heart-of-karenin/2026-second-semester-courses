The original proof of the Eilenberg–Zilber theorem did not use acyclic
models. Instead, it used the chain map ν of the last theorem, and a chain map
µ defined by a similar formula. One wrote down formulas to show that both
ν ◦µ and µ ◦ν were chain homotopic to identity maps.
