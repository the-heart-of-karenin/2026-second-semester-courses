The K¨unneth Theorem
Now we prove a basic theorem that enables us to compute the homology of
the tensor product of two chain complexes. As you might expect, the proof is
highly algebraic.

natural monomorphism of the tensor product Hp(C) ⊗Hq(C′) into the group
Hp+q(C ⊗C′).
