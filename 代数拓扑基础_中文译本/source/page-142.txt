Corollary 2.7.12 The Euclidean spaces Rn and Rm are not homeomorphic
if n̸ = m.
Proof: Suppose to the contrary that h is a homeomorphism of Rn with Rm.
Then, by restriction, h is a homeomorphism of Rn \ {0} with Rm \ {p} for
p = h(0) ∈Rm. The latter space is of course homeomorphic with Rm \ {0}. It
now follows from the preceding theorem that Sn−1 and Sm−1 are homotopy
equivalent. This cannot be true if n̸ = m because Hn−1(Sn−1) = Z while
Hn−1(Sm−1) = 0.
EXAMPLE 2.7.13 The wedge of two circles has the same homotopy type
as the letter θ. Let X be a doubly punctured elliptical region in the plane as
shown in Figure 2.7.5.
Figure 2.7.5
The sequence of arrows on the left side of this figure indicates how one
can collapse X to the wedge of two circles. The arrows on the right side show
