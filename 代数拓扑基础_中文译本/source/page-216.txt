Definition 4.3.8 Let f : (X, A) →(Y, B). If there is a map g : (Y, B) →
(X, A) such that f ◦g and g ◦f are homotopic to the appropriate identities
as maps of pairs, then we call f a homotopy equivalence, and we call g a
homotopy inverse for f.
