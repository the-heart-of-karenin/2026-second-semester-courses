∗Application: Local Homology Groups and

In this section, we define the local homology groups of a space X at a point x of
X, and we use these groups to prove several non-trivial facts about manifolds.
Throughout this section, let X denote a Hausdorff space.
This section will be assumed as background for Chapter 8. It is also used
in treating one of the examples in Section 4.12.
