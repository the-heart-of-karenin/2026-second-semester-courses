For the record, we define what we mean by a surface.
Definition 1.7.1 By a surface we mean a nonempty, connected Hausdorff
space, X, with a countable basis, such that each point of X has a neighborhood
homeomorphic with an open subset of R2.
In Section 4.8 we will define the notion of an m-manifold. According to
that definition, every surface is a 2-manifold, but because we require a surface
to be connected and have a countable basis, not every 2-manifold is a surface.
Convention. For convenience in notation, we shall henceforth delete the di-
mensional subscript p on the boundary operator ∂p, and rely on the context
to make clear which of these operators is intended.

other surfaces that can be constructed from a rectangle L by identifying its
edges appropriately. Thus we begin by proving certain facts about L itself.
