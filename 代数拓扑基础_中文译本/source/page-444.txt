Now we define a homomorphism in cohomology analogous to the map Θ
that appeared in the homology version of the K¨unneth theorem.
Definition 7.10.3 We define the tensor homomorphism for homology
Θ : Hp(C; R) ⊗Hq(C′; R) →Hm(C ⊗C′; R) ,
where m = p + q, as follows: Let ϕ and ψ be cocycles of dimensions p and q,
respectively. Then define
Θ({ϕ} ⊗{ψ}) = {θ(ϕ ⊗ψ)} .
Note that θ(ϕ ⊗ψ) is a cocycle, because
∂θ(ϕ ⊗ψ) = θ(δ(ϕ ⊗ψ)) = θ(δϕ ⊗ψ ± ϕ ⊗δ′ψ) ,
which vanishes, because ϕ and ψ are cocycles. A similar computation shows
that Θ is well-defined.
