Theorem 7.2.6 There are functors assigning to each short exact sequence of
abelian groups
0 −→A −→B −→C −→0 ,
and each abelian group G, two exact sequences:
0 ←Ext(A, G) ←Ext(B, G) ←Ext(C, G)
Hom(A, G) ←Hom(B, G) ←Hom(C, G) ←0 ,
0 →Hom(G, A) →Hom(G, B) →Hom(G, C)
Ext(G, A) →Ext(G, B) →Ext(G, C) →0 .
Exercise 4 should guide you through a proof of this theorem. The next defi-
nition is used in that proof:
Definition 7.2.7 Let G be an abelian group. An exact sequence
0 −→G −→H −→K −→0 ,
where H and K are divisible [see Definition 5.2.9], is called an injective reso-
lution of G.
