Set A0 = A. Lemma 1.12.7 tells us that if α(A0) does not divide every
entry of A0, then elementary operations can be applied to A0 to produce a
matrix A1 with α(A1) < α(A0). Repeating this process [no more than α(A0)
times] will yield a matrix Ar for which α(Ar) divides every element of Ar.
We will apply further elementary operations to Ar, but for convenience of
notation, we will continue to denote it by Ar.
Apply elementary operations to bring a minimal entry of Ar to the upper
left corner of the matrix and to make it positive. Because the minimal entry
divides all entries in its row and column, we can apply elementary operations
to make all the other entries in its row and column into zeros. Note that at
the end of this process, the entry in the upper left corner of Ar divides all
entries of the matrix. In particular, it divides all the entries of the submatrix
A′ obtained by omitting the first row and first column from Ar. Let bk+1 be
the entry in the upper left corner of Ar.
