Theorem 5.6.13 Let C be a free chain complex. If Hp(C) is free for all p,
then κ is an isomorphism for all p.
Proof: Let λ : C →E be as in the preceding lemma. Since the homology
of C is free, E is a free chain complex and Theorem 5.6.6 applies. Since the
chain map λ : C →E induces homology isomorphisms λ∗in all dimensions,
λ∗is an isomorphism. The fact that κ ◦λ∗is the identity implies that κ is an
isomorphism as well.
This last theorem says that if the homology of C is free in all dimensions,
then the cohomology group Hp(C; G) can be considered in a natural way to
be the dual group Hom (Hp(C), G) of the homology group Hp(C). We will
generalize this theorem in Chapter 7. All one in fact needs is for the single
group Hp−1(C) to be free.
