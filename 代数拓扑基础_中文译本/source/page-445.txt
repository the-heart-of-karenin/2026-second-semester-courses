Definition 7.10.4 Let X and Y be topological spaces. Suppose that m =
p + q. We define the cohomology cross product
× : Hp(X; R) ⊗Hq(Y ; R) →Hm(X × Y ; R)
to be the composite
Hp(X; R) ⊗Hq(Y ; R) Θ
−→Hm(S(X) ⊗S(Y ); R) ν∗
−→Hm(X × Y ; R) ,
where ν is the Eilenberg–Zilber chain equivalence of Theorem 7.9.5. The image
of αp ⊗βq under this homomorphism is denoted αp × βq.
Let us note that, as was the case with the cup product, the homomorphism
Θ can be defined for coefficients other than R. We give one such generalization
in the following definition:

In both these cases, one has an induced homomorphism Θ on the coho-
mology level, and thus a cohomology cross product operation as well. In one
case, the cross product is a homomorphism
× : Hp(X) ⊗Hq(Y ; G) →Hm(X × Y ; G) .
In the other case, it is an R-module homomorphism
× : Hp(X; R) ⊗R Hq(Y ; R) →Hm(X × Y ; R) .
