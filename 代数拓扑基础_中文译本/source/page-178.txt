Theorem 3.3.6 The preceding lemma holds for an arbitrary continuous map
h : (|K|, |K0|) →(|L|, |L0|).
We described the exact homology sequence of a pair in Theorem 3.2.8,
and we proved its existence in this section. There is also an exact homology
sequence of a triple, as stated in the next theorem. Exercise 1 asks you to
prove its existence.
Definition 3.3.7 Let X be a complex. If A is a subcomplex of X and B is a
subcomplex of A, then we call (X, A, B) a simplicial triple or simply a triple
if the context makes the meaning clear.
Theorem 3.3.8 (The exact homology sequence of a triple) Let X be
a complex. Let A be a subcomplex of X. Let B be a subcomplex of A. The
exact homology sequence of the triple is the following:
