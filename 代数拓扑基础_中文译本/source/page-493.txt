Our proof of this theorem used simplicial homology throughout. But the
fact that simplicial theory and singular theory are isomorphic, by an isomor-
phism that preserves cap products, means that this theorem holds for singular
theory as well. In particular, the isomorphism given by ⌢Γ depends only on
the homology class Γ, not on any particular triangulation involved.
