Definition 8.13.9 If J is a directed set, then a subset J0 of J is said to be
cofinal in J if for each α ∈J there exists a δ ∈J0 with α < δ.
Lemma 8.13.10 Suppose that one is given a direct system {Gα, fαβ} of
abelian groups and homomorphisms, indexed by the directed set J. If J0 is
cofinal in J, then J0 is a directed set, and inclusion induces an isomorphism
dir lim α∈J0Gα ∼= dir lim α∈JGα .
