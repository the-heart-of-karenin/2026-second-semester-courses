Now we shall prove excision in the general case. Let U ⊂Int A. Let
h : (K, K0) →(X, A)
k : (M, M0) →(X \ U, A \ U)
be triangulations of these respective pairs of spaces. Let X1 denote the closure
of X \A and let A1 = X1 ∩A. We assert that the pair (X1, A1) is triangulated
by both h and k. Refer to Figure 3.6.1, where the maps j0 and j1 both denote
inclusions.
To verify this assertion, note that the space |K| \ |K0| is the union of all
open simplices Int σ such that σ ∈K and σ̸ ∈K0. Then its closure C is the
polytope of the subcomplex of K consisting of all simplices σ of K that are
not in K0, and their faces. The image of the set C under the homeomorphism
h equals the closure of X \ A, which is X1. Therefore X1 is triangulated by
h. Since both X1 and A are triangulated by h, so is A1 = X1 ∩A. Similarly,
the closure of |M| \ |M0| is the polytope of a subcomplex of M, and its image
under k equals the closure of
(X \ U) \ (A \ U) = X \ A .
