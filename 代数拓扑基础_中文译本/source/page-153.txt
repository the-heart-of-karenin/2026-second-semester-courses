Let z be an n-cycle of S(K). Then z is a chain of the form
z = [w0, cm] + [w1, dm] ,
where cm, dm are chains of K and m = n −1. Here we are using the bracket

Assume that n > 1. Since z is a cycle,
0 = ∂z = cm −[w0, ∂cm] + dm −[w1, ∂dm] .
Restricting this chain to K, we obtain the equation cm + dm = 0. Hence
z = [w0, cm] −[w1, cm] .
Since r exchanges w0 and w1, we have
r#(z) = [w1, cm] −[w0, cm] = −z
as desired. A similar computation holds if n = 1.
