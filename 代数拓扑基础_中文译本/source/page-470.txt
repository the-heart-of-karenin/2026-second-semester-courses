8.4
The Dual Block Complex
For simplicity we are going to restrict ourselves in the next few sections to the
case of a homology n-manifold X. The techniques that we use will reappear
later when we consider the case of a relative homology manifold.
There is associated with the triangulated homology manifold X a certain
partition of X into subsets that are [almost] open cells such that X becomes
[almost] a regular cell complex. The subsets are not actual topological cells
but only homological cells. Since we cannot call them “cells,” we shall instead
call them “blocks.” And we shall call the collection of these blocks the dual
block decomposition of X. Just as there was with cell complexes, there is a
chain complex associated to this block complex that can be used to compute
the homology and cohomology of X. We will call it the “dual chain complex.”
It will be a crucial tool in proving our duality theorems.

proper face of σ1.
