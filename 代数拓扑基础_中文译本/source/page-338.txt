First, let us work on the level of chain complexes.
The Cohomology of a Chain Complex
Let C = {Cp, ∂} be a chain complex, and let G be an abelian group. We
define the p-dimensional cochain group of C, with coefficients in G, by
Cp(C; G) = Hom (Cp, G) .
We define the coboundary operator δ to be the dual of the boundary operator.
It follows that δ2 = 0. The family of groups and homomorphisms {Cp(C; G), δ}
is called the cochain complex of C with coefficients in G. As usual, the kernel
of the homomorphism
δ : Cp(C; G) →Cp+1(C; G)
is denoted Zp(C; G), its image is denoted Bp+1(C; G), and the cohomology
group of C in dimension p, with coefficients in G, is defined by the equation
Hp(C; G) = Zp(C; G)/Bp(C; G) .
