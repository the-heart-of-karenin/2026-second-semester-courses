We call ∆p the standard p-simplex. Note that, under this definition, ∆p−1 is

If X is a topological space, we define a singular p-simplex of X to be a
continuous map
T : ∆p →X .
The word “singular” is used to emphasize that T need not be an imbedding.
The map T could be a constant map for instance.
The free abelian group generated by the singular p-simplices of X is de-
noted by Sp(X) and is called the singular chain group of X in dimension p. By
our usual convention for free abelian groups [see Section 1.5], we shall denote
an element of Sp(X) by a formal linear combination, with integer coefficients,
of singular p-simplices.
