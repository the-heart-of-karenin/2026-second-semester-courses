Note that nowhere in the proof of this lemma did we assume that the chain
complexes involved were free or that they were non-negative. This lemma thus
has a much broader range of application than just to chain groups or simplicial
complexes. However, when applied to the simplicial chain groups C(K0) and
C(K) and C(K, K0), we obtain Theorem 3.2.8 for non-reduced homology as
an immediate consequence.
