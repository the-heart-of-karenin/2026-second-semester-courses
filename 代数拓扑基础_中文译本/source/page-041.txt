Direct Products and External Direct Sums
Let {Gα}α∈J be an indexed family of abelian groups. Their direct product

α∈J Gα is the group whose underlying set is the cartesian product of the sets
Gα and whose group operation is component-wise addition. Their external
direct sum G is the subgroup of the direct product consisting of all tuples
(gα)α∈J such that gα = 0α for all but finitely many values of α. [Here 0α is
the zero element of Gα.] The group G is sometimes also called the weak direct
product of the groups Gα.
The relation between internal and external direct sums is described as
follows: Suppose that G is the external direct sum of the groups {Gα}. Then

tuple (gα)α∈J for which gα = 0α for α different from β and gβ = g. Then
