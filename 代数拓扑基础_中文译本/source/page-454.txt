Proof: Let π1 : X × Y →X and π2 : X × Y →Y be the projection maps.
Step 1. First, we prove the formula when β and α′ are the unity elements of
their respective cohomology rings.
Let zp be a cocycle representing α, and let zq be a cocycle representing β′.
Then α × β′ is represented by zp × zq. We compute
⟨zp × zq, T⟩= ⟨zp, π1 ◦T ◦ℓ(ϵ0, ϵ1, . . . , ϵp)⟩· ⟨zq, π2 ◦T ◦ℓ(ϵp, ϵp+1, . . . , ϵm)⟩
= ⟨π#
1 (zp), T ◦ℓ(ϵ0, ϵ1, . . . , ϵp)⟩· ⟨π#
2 (zq), T ◦ℓ(ϵp, ϵp+1, . . . , ϵm)⟩
= ⟨π#
1 (zp) ⌣π#
2 (zq), T⟩.
Thus we have the desired formula
α × β′ = π∗
1(α) ⌣π∗
2(β′) = (α × 1Y ) ⌣(1X × β′) .
Step 2. Next, we prove the formula when β and β′ both equal the unity
element of their cohomology ring.
By naturality of cup products, we have
π∗
1(α) ⌣π∗
1(α′) = π∗
1(α ⌣α′) .
This last formula tells us that
(α × 1Y ) ⌣(α′ × 1Y ) = (α ⌣α′) × 1Y .
Step 3. Finally, we prove the general case.
We compute
(α × β) ⌣(α′ × β′) = (α × 1Y ) ⌣(1X × β) ⌣(α′ × 1Y ) ⌣(1X × β′)
= (−1)pq(α × 1Y ) ⌣(α′ × 1Y ) ⌣(1X × β) ⌣(1X × β′)
= (−1)pq(α ⌣α′) × (β ⌣β′) .
The preceding theorem enables us to compute the cup product of certain
elements of the cohomology ring H∗(X × Y ; R) in terms of the cup product
operations in H∗(X) and H∗(Y ). In the case where the K¨unneth theorem for
cohomology holds, we can state this fact more formally, as we do in Theo-
rem 7.11.8 below.
Definition 7.11.7 Given X and Y , we give the tensor product
H∗(X; R) ⊗R H∗(Y ; R)
the structure of a ring by defining
(α ⊗β) ⌣(α′ ⊗β′) = (−1)pq(α ⌣α′) ⊗(β ⌣β′) ,
if β ∈Hp(Y ; R) and α′ ∈Hq(X; R).
You can check that this operation is well-defined, and satisfies the axioms
for a ring. In particular, the signs come out right for associativity.
