EXAMPLE 3.2.9 Let K be the complex depicted in Figure 3.2.1, whose
polytope is a square region. Let K0 be the subcomplex whose polytope is
the boundary of the square. We know from Example 1.10.4 that H2(K, K0)
is infinite cyclic and is generated by the 2-chain γ that is the sum of the
2-simplices, oriented counterclockwise. Furthermore, H1(K0) is infinite cyclic
and is generated by the 1-chain s1 + s2 + s3 + s4, which happens to equal ∂γ.
Thus, in this particular case, the boundary homomorphism
∂∗: H2(K, K0) →H1(K0)
is an isomorphism.
