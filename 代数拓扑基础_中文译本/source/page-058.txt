Proof: As in the proof of Theorem 1.7.3, let g : |L| →|S| be the pasting map,
and let A = g(|Bd L|). As before, A is the wedge of two circles. Orient the 2-
simplices of S counterclockwise, and let γ be their sum. Orient the 1-simplices
of S arbitrarily. The statements (1) and (2) of the proof of Theorem 1.7.3
hold in this case too, since neither involves the particular identification on the
boundary. Because A is the wedge of two circles, (3) holds as well. The final
condition (4) is different; instead one has
(4) ∂γ = 2z1.
To see that (4) holds, one does a direct computation. For example, [a, b]
appears in ∂σ1 with coefficient −1 and in ∂σ2 with coefficient +1. On the
other hand, [a, d] appears in both ∂σ3 and ∂σ4 with coefficient +1. You can
check that similar conclusions apply to the other oriented 1-simplices in w1
and z1.
