Simplicial Cap Product
Just as with cup products, one needs a formula that holds for simplicial
theory if one wants to readily compute. By now it may be clear how to obtain
such a formula.
Definition 8.6.5 Given a complex K, choose a partial ordering of the vertices
of K that linearly orders the vertices of each simplex. We define a homomor-
phism
Cp(K; R) ⊗Cp+q(K; R) ⌢
−→Cq(K; R) ,
called the simplicial cap product, by the formula
cp ⌢([v0, v1, . . . , vp+q] ⊗α) = [v0, v1, . . . , vq] ⊗α · ⟨cp, [vq, vq+1, . . . , vp+q]⟩.
Theorem 8.6.6 The simplicial cap product satisfies the boundary formula
and the cup product formula of Theorem 8.6.2. It induces a homomorphism
in simplicial theory given by
Hp(K; R) ⊗Hp+q(K; R) →Hq(K; R)
that corresponds to the singular cap product under the standard isomorphism
between simplicial and singular theory.
