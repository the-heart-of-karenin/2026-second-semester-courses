Proof of Theorem 7.6.2: Choose a chain complex C′ as in the preceding
lemma. In view of Theorem 5.6.2, we can choose a chain map ϕ : C′ →C in-
ducing the given isomorphism Hi(C′) −→Hi(C). It follows from the universal
coefficient theorems already stated [or from Theorems 5.6.6 and 6.3.1] that ϕ
also induces isomorphisms
Hi(C′; G) ∼= Hi(C; G)
and
Hi(C′; G) ∼= Hi(C; G)
for all i and G. Now the universal coefficient sequences in question hold for C′.
Using the isomorphisms induced by ϕ, we can replace C′ by C in these exact
sequences.
It is a consequence of naturality that the resulting exact sequences for C
are independent of the choices of C′ and ϕ; a fact that we now prove.
