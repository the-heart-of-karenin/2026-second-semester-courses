Exercise 7 concerns the Bockstein homomorphism which we define next.
Definition 3.3.10 Given a complex K and a short exact sequence of abelian
groups
0 −→G →G′ −→G′′ −→0 ,
one has a short exact sequence of chain complexes
0 −→Cp(K; G) −→Cp(K; G′) −→Cp(K; G′′) −→0 .
That short exact sequence gives rise to a long exact sequence in homology.
The zig-zag homomorphism in this sequence is commonly denoted by
β∗: Hp(K; G′′) −→Hp−1(K; G)
and is called the Bockstein homomorphism associated with the given coefficient
sequence.
