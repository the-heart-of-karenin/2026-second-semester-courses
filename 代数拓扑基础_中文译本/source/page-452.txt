The chain maps λ# and ν are known to preserve augmentation, and they
are natural with respect to homomorphisms induced by continuous maps. One
checks readily that ω has the same properties. Consider the functors
G(X, Y ) = S(X × Y ) ,
G′(X, Y ) = S(Y ) ⊗S(X) ,
G(f, g) = (f × g)# ,
G′(f, g) = g# ⊗f# .
We have already shown that these functors are free and acyclic relative to
the collection of model objects M = {∆p, ∆q}. Therefore, any two natural
transformations of G to G′ are naturally chain homotopic. The chain maps
ω ◦ν and ν ◦λ# are two such natural transformations, as we noted. Therefore
they are chain homotopic.

We note that all three of these properties, suitably interpreted, hold for the
other version of the cross product. Anticommutativity (a) holds if one uses
(Z, G) coefficients on the right. Associativity (b) holds if two of the coefficients
groups are Z and one is G. The first statement in (c) holds if iY ∈H0(Y )
and αp ∈Hp(X; G), since then αp × 1Y ∈Hp(X × Y ; G) and the statement
makes sense. A similar remark applies to 1X × βq.
