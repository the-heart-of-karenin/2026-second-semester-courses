Everything depends on computing the homomorphism i∗. Since the retraction
r : |K| →C is a homotopy equivalence, it suffices to compute the homomor-
phism induced by the composite map r ◦i : |K0| →C, which collapses the
edge |K0| to the central circle C. In the case of the cylinder, this map has
degree 1. But in the case of the M¨obius band, the induced homomorphism
clearly equals multiplication by 2.
Thus H2(K, K0) = H1(K, K0) = 0 in the case of the cylinder. In the case
of the M¨obius band we have
H2(K, K0) = 0
and
H1(K, K0) ∼= Z/2 .
The central circle C represents the non-zero element of H1(K, K0) in this case.
So also does the chain D, which you may prove as an exercise.
