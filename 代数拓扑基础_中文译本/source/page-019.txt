EXAMPLE 1.2.2 In low dimensions, one can picture a simplex easily. A
0-simplex is a point, of course. The 1-simplex spanned by a0 and a1 consists
of all points of the form

with 0 ≤t ≤1, so it is simply the line segment joining a0 and a1. That line
segment can be written a0a1.
