Now we apply these results to lens spaces. Recall that if n and k are rela-
tively prime positive integers, then the lens space X = L(n, k) is a compact,
connected, orientable, triangulable 3-manifold. Furthermore, X has the struc-
ture of a CW complex. Its cellular chain complex D(X) is infinite cyclic in
each dimension i = 0, 1, 2, 3. And the boundary operator is either 0 or multi-
plication by n. It follows that
Hi(X; Z/n) ∼= Z/n ∼= Hi(X; Z/n)
for i = 1, 2, 3.
