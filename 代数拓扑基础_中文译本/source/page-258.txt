3. (a) Consider S3 as R3 with a point at infinity adjoined. Let A,
B1, B2, and B3 be the simple closed curves in S3 pictured in
Figure 4.9.6. The map
H1(Bi) −→H1(S3 \ A)
induced by inclusion is a homomorphism of infinite cyclic
groups, so it equals multiplication by di, where di is well-defined
up to sign. The integer di measures how many times Bi links
A. What is it in each case? Similarly, determine the integer
corresponding to the homomorphism
H1(A) −→H1(S3 \ Bi)
induced by inclusion. Can you formulate a conjecture?
