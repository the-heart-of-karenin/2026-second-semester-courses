Order the vertices of T alphabetically. Using this ordering, we compute the
value of w1 ⌣z1 on each oriented 2-simplex σ. Note that ⟨w1 ⌣z1, σ⟩= 0
unless a face of σ is in the carrier of w1 and a face of σ is also in the carrier of
z1. Thus the only possible non-zero values occur when σ is one of the simplices
[g, h, i] or [h, i, j]. We compute
⟨w1 ⌣z1, [g, h, i]⟩= ⟨w1, [g, h]⟩· ⟨z1, [h, i]⟩= 1 · 1 = 1 ,
⟨w1 ⌣z1, [h, i, j]⟩= ⟨w1, [h, i]⟩· ⟨z1, [i, j]⟩= (−1) · 0 = 0 .
Thus we have w1 ⌣z1 = [g, h, i]∗. Now the orientation of [g, h, i] is clockwise.
Therefore, in terms of our standard generators, α ⌣β = −Λ.
A similar computation shows that
⟨z1 ⌣w1, [g, h, i]⟩= ⟨z1, [g, h]⟩· ⟨w1, [h, i]⟩= 0 · (−1) = 0 ,
⟨z1 ⌣w1, [h, i, j]⟩= ⟨z1, [h, i]⟩· ⟨w1, [i, j]⟩= 1 · 1 = 1 ,
so that z1 ⌣w1 = [h, i, j]∗. Thus we have β ⌣α = Λ, as anticommutivity
would have led us to expect.
A similar direct computation shows that α ⌣α = 0 and β ⌣β = 0.
Alternatively, we note that w1 is cohomologous to the cochain y1 pictured

another face in the carrier of y1, necessarily w1 ⌣y1 = 0. Hence α ⌣α = 0.
A similar argument shows that β ⌣β = 0.
