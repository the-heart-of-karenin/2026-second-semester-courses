Definition 8.10.2 Let (X, A) be a compact triangulated relative homology
n-manifold. We say that (X, A) is orientable if it is possible to orient all the
n-simplices σi of X not in A so that their sum γ =  σi is a cycle of (X, A).
Such a cycle γ will be called an orientation cycle for (X, A).
