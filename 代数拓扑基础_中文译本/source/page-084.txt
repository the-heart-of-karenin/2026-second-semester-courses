Now we prove the basic theorem concerning subgroups of free abelian
groups that we stated in Section 1.5.
Proof of Theorem 1.5.2: Given a free abelian group F of rank n, we know
from Lemma 1.12.1 that any subgroup R is free of rank r ≤n. Consider the
inclusion homomorphism j : R →F, and choose bases a1, a2, . . . , ar for R
and e1, e2, . . . , en for F relative to which the matrix of j is in the normal form
of the preceding Theorem 1.12.4. Because j is a monomorphism, this normal
form has no zero columns. Thus, j(ai) = biei for i = 1, 2, . . . , r, where bi ≥1
and b1 | b2 | . . . | br. Since j(ai) = ai, it follows that b1e1, b2e2, . . . , brer is a
basis for R
