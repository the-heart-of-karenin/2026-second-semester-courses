To define Θ and Φ we proceed as follows. For each simplex τ ∈K′, let στ
be the simplex of K of least dimension such that τ ⊂στ. Then, if t is a face of
τ, we have σt ⊂στ. For since σt and στ both contain t, their intersection also
contains t. Since σt has minimal dimension, it must equal this intersection.
We define
Θ(τ) = K(στ) ,
Φ(τ) = K′(στ) .
Both complexes are acyclic. The inclusion condition follows from the fact that
if τ ≻t then σt ⊂στ. See Figure 2.5.1, which illustrates these carriers in the
case that K′ = sd K.
