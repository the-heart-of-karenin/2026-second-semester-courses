Review of Abelian Groups
In this section we review some results from algebra that we shall be using—
specifically, facts about abelian groups.
We write abelian groups with additive notation, so 0 denotes the identity
element and −g denotes the inverse of g. If n is a positive integer, then ng
denotes the n-fold sum g + g + · · · + g and (−n)g denotes n(−g).
