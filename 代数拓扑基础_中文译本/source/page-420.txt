7.6
∗Other Universal Coefficient Theorems
We have stated two universal coefficient theorems that express the homology
and cohomology groups with arbitrary coefficients in terms of homology with
integer coefficients. One might ask whether for this purpose cohomology and
homology are symmetric in some sense—that is, whether these same groups
can be expressed in terms of cohomology with integer coefficients. In fact,
there are two short exact sequences that do this. They do not hold in complete
generality, but apply only if the homology groups Hi(C) are finitely generated.
We consider these theorems now. We will be using the results of Section 5.7.
The exact sequences in question are derived by reindexing the groups of the
cochain complex {Hom (Ci, Z), δ} so as to make it into a chain complex, and
then applying the universal coefficient theorems we have already considered.
The difficulty with this approach is that the group Hom (Ci, Z) is not in general
free, so some restrictive hypotheses are needed.
