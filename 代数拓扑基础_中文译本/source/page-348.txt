Corollary 5.6.3 Suppose that {C, ϵ} and {C′, ϵ′} are free augmented chain
complexes. If γ : Hp(C) →Hp(C′) is a homomorphism defined for all p, then
γ is induced by an augmentation-preserving chain map ϕ : C →C′.
Proof: Consider the augmented chain complexes obtained from C and C′.
They have Z as their (−1)-dimensional groups and ϵ, ϵ′, respectively, as the
boundary operators from dimension 0 to dimension −1. We define β to equal
the identity in dimension −1 [where the homology vanishes], and to be any
homomorphism of the groups of cycles inducing γ in other dimensions. The
preceding theorem applies. The resulting chain map ϕ will automatically pre-
serve augmentation.
