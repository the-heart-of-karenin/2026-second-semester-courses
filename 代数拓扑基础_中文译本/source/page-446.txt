The K¨unneth Theorem for Cohomology with Integer Coefficients
We will now prove the K¨unneth theorem for cohomology with integer coeffi-
cients. The idea of the proof is to re-index the groups Hom (Cp, Z) so as to
make Hom (C, Z) into a chain complex, and then apply the K¨unneth theorem
that we have already proved. One difficulty is that Hom (Cp, Z) is in general
not free. So some restrictive hypotheses are needed.
An additional difficulty is that, by definition, the cohomology of C ⊗C′ is
computed from the cochain complex Hom ((C ⊗C′)m, Z), and that is not the
same as the cochain complex
Hom (C, Z) ⊗Hom (C′, Z)
