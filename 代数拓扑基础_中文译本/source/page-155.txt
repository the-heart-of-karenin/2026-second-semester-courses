Let sd K be its first barycentric subdivision. Let f : sd K →K
be the simplicial map specified by F(ai) = a2i, where the index
2i is reduced modulo 8 if necessary. Show that the map f has
degree 2.
(b) Consider S1 as the set of complex numbers of unit modulus.
Show that the map h : S1 →S1 given by h(z) = z2 has degree
2. [Hint: By radial projection, h induces a map of |K| to itself
that is homotopic to the simplicial map f of part (a).]
(c) If n is any positive integer, show that the map k : S1 →S1
given by k(z) = zn has degree n.
