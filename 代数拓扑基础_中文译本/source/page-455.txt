Theorem 7.11.8 If Hi(X) is finitely generated for all i, then the cohomology
cross product defines a monomorphism of rings
× : H∗(X) ⊗H∗(Y ) →H∗(X × Y ) .
If F is a field, then
× : H∗(X; F) ⊗F H∗(Y ; F) →H∗(X × Y ; F)
defines an isomorphism of algebras.
