If K0 = ∅, then we call K a pseudo n-manifold.
In Section 1.6 in which we defined the homology groups, we required that
the simplices of a complex be oriented, but the orientation of one simplex
need not have any effect on the choice of orientation for any other simplex. In
fact, it is useful to know whether or not the top dimensional simplices can be
coherently oriented.
Definition 5.4.6 Let (K, K0) be a relative pseudo n-manifold.
(1) Suppose that σ̸ = σ′ are n-simplices of K, not in K0, and the
sequence of n-simplices of K, not in K0,
σ = σ0, σ1, . . . , σk = σ′
such that σi ∩σi+1 is an (n −1)-simplex not in K0, for each i,
has no repetitions. [Exercise 5(a) asks you to show such a sequence
exists.] Once σ is oriented, there are unique orientations of each σi
such that ∂σi−1 + ∂σi has coefficient 0 on σi−1 ∩σi for each i. The
resulting orientation of σk = σ′ is said to be induced by the given
orientation of σ relative to the given sequence.
(2) Let σ be an n-simplex of K that is not in K0. Fix an orientation
for σ. If, for every σ′̸ = σ, the orientation of σ′ induced by that of
σ is independent of the sequence joining them, then (K, K0) is said
to be orientable. Otherwise, (K, K0) is said to be non-orientable.
