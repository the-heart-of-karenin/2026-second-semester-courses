In this section we present several applications of homology theory to classical
problems of geometry and topology. The theorems that we prove here will
be generalized in the next section. That is when we will treat the celebrated
Lefschetz fixed point theorem.
Definition 2.9.1 Let n ≥1. Let f : Sn →Sn be a continuous map. If α is
one of the two generators of the infinite cyclic group Hn(Sn), then f∗(α) = dα
for some integer d. The integer d is independent of the choice of generator
since f∗(−α) = d(−α). It is called the degree of the map f and is denoted
deg f
