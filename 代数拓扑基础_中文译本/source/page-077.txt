EXAMPLE 1.11.2 Let us compute the homology of the torus and Klein
bottle using the rational numbers Q as coefficients. The same arguments as
before apply, but the end results are different. For the torus, one has
H1(T; Q) ∼= Q ⊕Q ,
H2(T; Q) ∼= Q .
For the Klein bottle, one has
H1(S; Q) ∼= Q ,
H2(S; Q) = 0 .
For Q coefficients, the cycle z1 bounds the chain 1/2 γ.

1. Compute the homology of P2 with Z/2 coefficients and with Q co-
efficients.
2. Show that homology with Q coefficients suffices to distinguish
among S2 and the connected sums P2 # P2 # · · · # P2 and
T # T # · · · # T.
3. Compute the homology of the Klein bottle S with Z/3 coefficients
and with Z/4 coefficients.
4. Compute the homology of the k-fold dunce cap with Z/n coefficients
and with Q coefficients. [See Exercise 6 of Section 1.7.]
5. Let (K, K0) be the pair consisting of the M¨obius band and its edge.
Compute Hi(K, K0; Z/2) and Hi(K, K0; Q). [See Exercise 2 of Sec-
tion 1.10.]
