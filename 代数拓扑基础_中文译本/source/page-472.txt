Keep these two examples in mind as we prove the following theorem.
Theorem 8.4.6 Let X be a locally finite simplicial complex that consists
entirely of n-simplices and their faces. Let σ be a k-simplex of X. Then:
(a) The dual blocks are disjoint and their union is |X|.
(b) D(σ) is the polytope of a subcomplex of sd X of dimension n−k.
(c) ˙D(σ) is the union of all blocks D(τ) for which τ has σ as a
proper face. These blocks have dimension less than n −k.
(d) D(σ) equals the cone | ˙D(σ) ∗σ|.
(e) If Hi(X, X \ σ) ∼= Z for i = n and vanishes otherwise, then
(D(σ), ˙D(σ)) has the homology of an n−k cell modulo its boundary.
Proof: (a) The open simplices of sd X are disjoint, and each one lies in
precisely one block D(σ)—namely the one such that σ is its final vertex.
