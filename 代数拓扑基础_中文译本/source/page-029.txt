Corollary 1.3.19 Let ∆N denote the complex consisting of an N-simplex
and its faces. If K is a finite complex, then K is isomorphic to a subcomplex
of ∆N for some N.
Proof: Let v0, v1, . . . , vN be the vertices of K. Choose a0, a1, . . . , aN to be
geometrically independent points in RN, and let ∆N consist of the N-simplex
they span, along with all its faces. The vertex map f(vi) = ai induces an
isomorphism with a subcomplex of ∆N.
