Corollary 7.8.5 Let C, C′, D, and D′ be chain complexes with C and D free.
If ϕ : C →D and ϕ′ : C′ →D′ are chain maps that induce homology isomor-
phisms in all dimensions, then ϕ ⊗ϕ′ : C ⊗C′ →D ⊗D′ induces homology
isomorphisms in all dimensions.
Proof: We apply naturality of the K¨unneth sequence and the Steenrod five
lemma [Lemma 3.3.4].
