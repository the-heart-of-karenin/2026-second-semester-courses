St(w, KN0+1) ⊂St(v, KN0)

St(w, KN0+1) ⊂St(v, KN0) ⊂Av

Now we prove (∗∗) in the case N > N0 + 1. If w′ is a vertex of KN lying

result of the preceding paragraph.
Step 4: We now complete the proof. Let λ be a Lebesgue number for the
open covering A of |K|. Consider the space Q. It is the polytope of a sub-
complex J of KN0+1. In forming the subdivision KN0+2, each simplex of J is

as a subcomplex. Repeating the argument, we see that in general KN0+1+M
has sdM J as a subcomplex.
Choose M large enough that each simplex of sdM J has diameter less than
