Definition 1.3.15 A simplicial complex K is said to be locally finite if each
vertex of K belongs to only finitely many simplices of K. Said differently, a
complex is locally finite if and only if each closed star St v is the polytope of
a finite subcomplex of K.
Lemma 1.3.16 The complex K is locally finite if and only if the space |K|
is locally compact.
Proof: Suppose K is locally finite. Given x ∈|K|, it lies in St v for some
vertex v of K. Since St v is a compact set, |K| is locally compact. We leave
the converse as an exercise.
