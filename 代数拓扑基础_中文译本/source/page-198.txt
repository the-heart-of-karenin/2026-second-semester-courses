EXAMPLE 3.7.8 Consider the category of pairs of spaces and pairs of
maps. Let G and H be the functors
G(X, Y ) = X × Y ;
G(f, g) = f × g ,
H(X, Y ) = Y × X;
H(f, g) = g × f .
Given (X, Y ), let T(X,Y ) be the homeomorphism of X × Y with Y × X that
switches coordinates. Then T is a natural equivalence of G with H.
