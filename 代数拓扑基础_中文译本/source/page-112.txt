Proof: Let f, g be simplicial approximations to h. Given σ ∈K0, choose

conclusion, we have
h(St(v, K0))
⊂
h(St(v, K)) ∩h(|K0|)
⊂
St(f(v), L) ∩|L0|
=
St(f(v), L0) .

The next definition is used in Exercise 2. It will be seen again in Section 2.7.
Definition 2.2.8 A homotopy between two maps f : X →Y and h : X →Y
is a continuous map F : X ×I →Y , where I = [0, 1], such that F(x, 0) = f(x)
and F(x, 1) = h(x) for all x ∈X.
The particular homotopy defined in equation (∗) below is called the
straight-line homotopy between f and h.
