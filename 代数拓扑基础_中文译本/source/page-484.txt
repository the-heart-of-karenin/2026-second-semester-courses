Theorem 8.6.2 The cap product is bilinear and is natural with respect to
continuous maps. It satisfies the boundary formula
∂(dp ⌢cp+q) = (−1)q(δdp ⌢cp+q) + dp ⌢∂cp+q ,
and is related to the cup product by the formula
cp ⌢(dq ⌢ep+q+r) = (cp ⌣dq) ⌢ep+q+r .

These formulas in fact hold for all three versions of the cap product. In the
case of the coefficient pairing G ⊗Z →G, one must add the proviso that the
cup product formula makes sense. This will occur if two of cp, dq, and ep+q+r
have coefficients in Z and the other has coefficients in G.
