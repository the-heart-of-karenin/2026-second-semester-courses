One can ask what sort of formula holds for ∂Dc when c is an arbitrary
chain. The answer is clear from the definition. Given the oriented simplex σ, we
compute ∂Dσ = g#(σ) −Dv −f#(σ) + Dw; see Figure 1.13.3 for verification.
That is,
∂(Dσ) = g#(σ) −f#(σ) −D(∂σ) .
(∗∗)
This same formula holds for the other simplices of K, as you can check.
