Theorem 5.10.2 Given an ordering of the vertices of the complex K, the cor-
responding simplicial cup product is bilinear and associative. The cochain z0
whose value is 1 on each vertex of K acts as a unity element. The coboundary
formula
δ(cp ⌣cq) = (δcp) ⌣cq + (−1)pcp ⌣(δcq)
of Theorem 5.9.5 holds also for the simplicial cup product.
If η : Cp(K) →Sp(|K|) is the chain map of Section 4.7, determined by the
given ordering, then its dual η carries the singular cup product to the simplicial
cup product.
Proof: The proofs are straightforward. Only the coboundary formula requires
comment. One can prove it by using the same computations used in proving
Theorem 5.9.5; only small changes of notation are needed.
As an alternative argument, one can use the fact that η carries basis el-
ements to basis elements, η is injective, and its image is a direct summand
in Sp(|K|). Hence its dual η is surjective. Given simplicial cochains cp and
cq, they can thus be pulled back to singular cochains of |K|, say c p and cq.
We know that the coboundary formula holds in singular theory for c p ⌣cq.
Since η preserves both cup products and coboundaries, the same coboundary
formula must hold in simplicial theory for cp ⌣cq.
