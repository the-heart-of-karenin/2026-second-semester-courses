EXAMPLE 8.8.4 Let us consider the torus once again. By Theorem 8.8.2,
there is a basis α1, α2 for H1(T) and a basis β1, β2 for H1(T) such that
αi ⌣βj = δijΛ where Λ is a generator of H2(T). The two “picket fence”
cocycles w1 and z1 pictured in Figure 8.8.1 give us a basis for H1(T) and
the indicated cocycle σ∗generates H2(T). Recall that z1 ⌣w1 = σ∗. The
conclusion of the theorem holds if we let α1 and α2 be the cohomology classes
of z1 and w1 respectively. And we let β1 and β2 be the cohomology classes
of w1 and −z1 respectively. Further, we let Λ be the cohomology class of σ∗.
These computations were carried out in Example 5.10.5.
The proof of the theorem tells us that this is the way things ought to work.
