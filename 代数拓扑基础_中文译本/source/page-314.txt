EXERCISES
1. Show that Pn is homeomorphic to the quotient space of B3 obtained
by identifying x with −x for each x ∈Sn−1.
2. (a) Show that Pn is an n-manifold and CP n is a 2n-manifold.
(b) Show more generally that if a finite-dimensional CW complex
is homogeneous, then it is a manifold.
3. Let A be a closed region in the plane bounded by a regular n-sided
polygon. Let B be the suspension of A. Describe L(n, k) as a quo-
tient space of B. Conclude that B3 and L(n, k) can be triangulated
so that the quotient map is simplicial.
