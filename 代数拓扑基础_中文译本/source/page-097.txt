Theorem 1.13.11 Let f, g : (K, K0) →(L, L0) be contiguous as maps of
pairs. Then for all p there is a homomorphism
D : Cp(K, K0) →Cp+1(L, L0)
such that ∂D + D∂= g# −f#. It follows that f∗and g∗are equal as maps
of relative homology groups.
Proof: The chain homotopy D constructed in the preceding proof of Theo-
rem 1.13.9 automatically maps Cp(K0) into Cp+1(L0). For if σ ∈K0, then the
complex L(σ) is by definition a subcomplex of L0. Given σ, the chain Dσ is
carried by L(σ); therefore, D maps Cp(K0) into Cp+1(L0). Then D induces
the required homomorphism of the relative chain groups.
