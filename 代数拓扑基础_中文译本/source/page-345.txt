Similarly, if h : X →Y is continuous, then the inverse image of a compact
subset of Y , while closed, need not be compact. Again it may be convenient

Definition 5.5.5 A map h : X →Y is said to be proper if for each compact
subset D of Y , the set h−1(D) is a compact subset of X. A proper homotopy
is a homotopy that is itself a proper map.
