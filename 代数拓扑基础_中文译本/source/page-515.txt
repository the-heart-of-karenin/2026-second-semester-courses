Corollary 8.10.5 Let (X, A) be a compact, triangulated relative homology
n-manifold with |X| \ |A| connected. If σ and σ′ are two n-simplices of X not
in A, then there is a sequence
σ = σ0, σ1, . . . , σk = σ′
of n-simplices not in A such that σi ∩σi+1 is an (n −1)-simplex not in A for
each i.
Proof: We define two n-simplices of X not in A to be equivalent if there is
such a sequence joining them. The sum of the members of any one equivalence
class is a relative cycle of (X, A) with Z/2 coefficients. We conclude that there
is only one equivalence class, since
Hn(X, A; Z/2) ∼= H0(|X| \ |A|; Z/2) ∼= Z/2 .
Corollary 8.10.6 Let (X, A) be a compact, triangulated relative homology
n-manifold. Assume that |X| \ |A| is connected. Then Hn(X, A) ∼= Z if (X, A)
is orientable and Hn(X, A) = 0 if (X, A) is non-orientable.
