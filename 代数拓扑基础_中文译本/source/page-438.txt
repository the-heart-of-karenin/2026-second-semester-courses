The Eilenberg–Zilber Theorem
Now we show that, for an arbitrary pair of topological spaces, the chain com-
plex S(X) ⊗S(Y ) can be used to compute the singular homology of X × Y .
The crucial tool for the proof is the acyclic model theorem.
