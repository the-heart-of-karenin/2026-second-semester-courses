The Universal Coefficient Theorem for
Cohomology
We now show how the homology groups of a space determine the cohomology
groups. The answer is expressed in the form of a short exact sequence involving
the groups Hp, Hp, and Hp−1, and the functors Hom and Ext.
We already know that if C is a free chain complex, then there is a natural
exact sequence
0 ←−Hom (Hp(C), G) κ
←−Hp(C; G) ←−ker κ ←−0 .
