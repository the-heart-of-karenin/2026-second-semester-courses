The Exact Homology Sequence
The relationship between the absolute and relative homology groups is ex-
pressed by what is called the “exact homology sequence of a pair.” In the
present section, we shall study exact sequences of groups (usually abelian
groups), and we shall define the exact homology sequence of a pair.
