Corollary 8.14.2 Let n > 1. Let M be a compact, connected, triangulable
n −1 manifold. Suppose that h : M →Sn is an imbedding. Then M is
orientable, and Sn \ h(M) has precisely two path components of which h(M)
is the common boundary.
