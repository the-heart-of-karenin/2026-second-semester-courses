Elements of Algebraic Topology
EXERCISES
1. (a) If r : X →A is a retraction and X is Hausdorff, then show that
A is closed in X.
(b) Let r : |K| →|K0| be a retraction, where K0 is a subcomplex
of K. Show that r∗: Hp(K) →Hp(K0) is surjective and the
homomorphism j∗induced by the inclusion j : |K0| →|K| is
injective.
(c) Show that there is no retraction r : Bn →Sn−1.
2. (a) Show that there is a retraction of the Klein bottle S onto the
embedded circle A pictured in Figure 2.6.1. But there is no
retraction of S onto the circle C.
(b) Show that there is no retraction of the projective plane onto
the embedded circle C pictured in Figure 2.6.2.
3. Determine whether there are retractions of the torus onto the tube
A depicted in Figure 2.6.3. Also onto the disc B and the circle C.
4. Determine all the spaces X such that there is a retraction from the
circle C onto X.
