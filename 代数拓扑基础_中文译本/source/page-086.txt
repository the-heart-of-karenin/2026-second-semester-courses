Using Step 1, choose Vp so that Zp = Vp ⊕Wp. Then we have a decomposition

Theorem 1.12.10 The homology groups of a finite complex K are effectively
computable.
Proof: By the preceding theorem, there is a decomposition
Cp(K) = Up ⊕Vp ⊕Wp
where Zp = Vp ⊕Wp is the group of p-cycles and Wp is the group of weak
p-boundaries. Now we have
Hp(K) = Zp/Bp ∼= Vp ⊕(Wp/Bp) ∼= (Zp/Wp) ⊕(Wp/Bp) .
The group Zp/Wp is free and the group Wp/Bp is a torsion group; computing
Hp(K) thus reduces to computing these two groups.
