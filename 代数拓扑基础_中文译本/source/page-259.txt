4.10
The Fundamental Group
In this section we will give a brief introduction to homotopy theory, and look
particularly at the fundamental group of a space. One of the motivations for
homotopy theory is identifying and classifying the “holes” in a topological
space. The annulus has a hole in its center, and so does the unit sphere. See
Figure 4.10.1. But one has a sense that these two holes are different in nature.
They are, perhaps, holes of different dimensions. Homotopy theory helps us
to sort out these ideas.
The basis for homotopy theory is to consider the set of all loops in a given
topological space X. Here, a loop means a continuous function γ : [0, 1] →X
such that γ(0) = γ(1). See Figure 4.10.2
