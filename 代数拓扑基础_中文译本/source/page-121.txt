Lemma 2.4.6 Let K0 be a subcomplex of K.
(a) If τ is a simplex of sd(K/K0), then τ is of the form
τ = σ1σ2 · · · σqv0v1 · · · vp ,
where s = v0v1 · · · vp is a simplex of K0 and σ1, σ2, · · · , σq are sim-
plices of K not in K0 and σ1 ≻σ2 ≻· · · ≻σq ≻s.
(b) Either v0v1 · · · vp or σ1σ2 · · · σq may be missing from this expres-
sion. The simplex τ is disjoint from |K0| if and only if v0v1 · · · vp is
missing. In this case τ is a simplex of sd K.
Proof: (a) The result is true if τ is in J0. In general, let τ be a simplex of
Jp+1, not in Jp. Then either τ belongs to K0, in which case τ is of the form
v0v1 · · · vr, or τ belongs to one of the cones σ ∗Jσ. Now each simplex of Jσ
has the form σ1σ2 · σqv0v2 · · · vr by the induction hypothesis, where σ ≻σ1.
Then τ has the form σσ1σ2 · · · σqv0v2 · · · vr as desired.
(b) Let τ = σ1σ2 · · · σqv0v2 · · · vr. If v0v1 · · · vp is not missing from this ex-
pression, then τ intersects |K0| in v0v1 · · · vp at least. Conversely, if the set
τ ∩|K0| is nonempty, then it contains a face of τ and hence a vertex of τ.
Since none of the points σ1, σ2, . . . , σq is in |K0|, the term v0v1 · · · vp cannot
be missing.
To prove the general simplicial approximation theorem, we need to show
that, given an arbitrary continuous map h : |K| →|L|, there is a subdivision
K′ of K such that h satisfies the star condition relative to K′ and L. This is
equivalent to the statement that if A is the open covering of K defined by
A = {h−1(St(w, L)) : w is a vertex of L} ,
then there is a subdivision of K′ of K such that the collection of open stars
B = {St(v, K′) : v is a vertex of K′}
refines A. [Recall that a collection B refines a collection A if, for each element
B ∈B, there is an element A ∈A that contains B.]
