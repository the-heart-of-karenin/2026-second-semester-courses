Theorem 2.4.9 (The general simplicial approximation theorem) Let
K and L be complexes. Let h : |K| →|L| be a continuous map. There exists a
subdivision K′ of K such that h has a simplicial approximation f : K′ →L.
Proof: Let A be the covering of |K| by the open sets h−1(St(w, L)) as w
ranges over the vertices of L. Choose a subdivision K′ of K whose closed
stars refine A. Then h satisfies the star condition relative to K′ and L.
