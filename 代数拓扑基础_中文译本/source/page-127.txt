Definition 2.5.2 Let K′ be a subdivision of K. If σ is a simplex of K, let
K(σ) denote the subcomplex of K consisting of σ and its faces. Let K′(σ)
denote the subcomplex of K′ whose polytope is σ.
Theorem 2.5.3 (The algebraic subdivision theorem) Let K′ be a sub-
division of K. There is a unique augmentation-preserving chain map
λ : C(K) →C(K′)
such that λ(σ) is carried by K′(σ) for each σ. If g : K′ →K is a simplicial
approximation to the identity, then λ and g# are chain-homotopy inverses.
Hence λ∗and g∗are isomorphisms.
