We remark that in the preceding proof we did not need the full strength of
the hypothesis of the excision axiom. We did not use the fact that U ⊂Int A,
nor that U is open. Thus for simplicial theory on the class of triangulable
pairs, the following stronger version of the excision axiom holds:
Theorem 3.6.3 (Excision in simplicial theory) Let A be a subspace of
X. Let U be a subset of X such that U ⊂Int A. If both of the pairs (X, A)
and (X \ U, A \ U) are triangulable, then inclusion induces an isomorphism
Hp(X \ U, A \ U) ∼= Hp(X, A) .
The compact support axiom states, roughly speaking, that every homol-
ogy class is compactly supported. It is also true that every homology relation
between such classes is compactly supported. More precisely, one has the fol-
lowing useful result, which we shall verify directly for simplicial theory. It may
also be derived from the axioms. See the exercises below.
