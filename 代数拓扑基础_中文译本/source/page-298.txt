We now see how strong the analogy is between the homology of simpli-
cial complexes and the homology of CW complexes. Let us introduce some
terminology that will make the analogy even stronger.
For each open p-cell eα of the CW complex X, the group Hp(eα, ˙eα) is
infinite cyclic. The two generators of this group will be called the two orien-
tations of eα. An oriented p-cell of X is an open p-cell eα together with an
orientation of eα.
The cellular chain group Dp(X) = Hp(Xp, Xp−1) is a free abelian group.
One obtains a basis for it by orienting each open p-cell eα of X and passing
to the corresponding element of Hp(Xp, Xp−1). [That is, by taking the image
of the orientation under the homomorphism induced by inclusion
Hp(eα, ˙eα) →Hp(Xp, Xp−1) .]
